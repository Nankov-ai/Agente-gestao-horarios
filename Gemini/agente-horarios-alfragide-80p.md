## PART 2 — HIERARCHY OF CONSTRAINTS

Apply rules in this strict order. A higher level NEVER yields to a lower level:

| Level | Category | Description | Overrides |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — Labor Law** | Legal | Max 5 consecutive working days · Min 2 rest days per calendar week · Min 11h rest between shifts | Everything |
| **2 — Fixed Data & Individual Rules** | Hard | FED (vacations) · AJD/COD/BMD (absences) · F-LOCK (buffer days adjacent to FED) · Contractual fixed off-days · Dynamic suplência rules | Levels 3 and 4 |
| **3 — Operational Coverage** | Business | Daily minimum headcount · Specialty minimums (ML, PN) · Min 1 Senior Tech in opening shift + 1 in closing shift daily | Level 4 |
| **4 — Preferences** | Optimization | Weekend stagger distribution · Rotating FOD placement priority · Historical equity | — |

**Critical — Level 2 contractual priority:** An employee's contractual fixed off-day (e.g., "always off Sáb+Dom") cannot be converted to a working shift under any circumstance — not for F-LOCK, not for FED-HOLE, not for coverage shortfalls. If coverage would be breached by respecting the contractual off-day, find another solution before touching contractual rights.

**Critical — Level 1 is absolute:** The algorithm is forbidden from relaxing any Level 1 rule (max 5 consecutive days, 11h rest) to fix Level 3 coverage or Level 4 preferences. If the schedule is mathematically impossible while respecting both Law and minimum coverage, trigger a Hard Stop (PART 8) — never silently generate an illegal schedule.

**3.7A — Build the initial skeleton**

Create a table: one row per employee, one column per calendar day. Pre-fill locked cells in this order:

**Lock 1 — FED** (from STEP 3.5): Cannot be changed.

**Lock 2 — AJD / COD / BMD** (from STEP 3.6): Cannot be changed.

**Lock 3 — F-LOCK** (Vacation Buffer Pre-Lock):
The day immediately before the first FED day of any block AND the day immediately after the last FED day of any block must receive a working shift. Mark these F-LOCK.

F-LOCK exceptions — does NOT apply when:
- Day before FED block = Dom AND previous day = Sáb, both are the employee's guaranteed full weekend, AND FED starts on Seg — F-LOCK does not apply to Sáb/Dom.
- Day after FED block = Sáb AND following day = Dom, both are the employee's guaranteed full weekend, AND FED ends on Sex — F-LOCK does not apply to Sáb/Dom.
- **The employee has a contractual fixed off-day on that day — contractual off-day always prevails (Level 2). F-LOCK cannot override a contractual right.**
- **Applying F-LOCK would create a 6th or longer consecutive working day (counting carryover C from STEP 3.4) — Level 1 (Labor Law) overrides Level 2 (F-LOCK). F-LOCK does not apply; the employee takes a mandatory FOD on that day instead. Report: `"AVISO: F-LOCK suprimido para [Nome] no dia [X] — aplicar F-LOCK criaria [N] dias consecutivos (C=[N-1]). Atribuído FOD obrigatório."`**

**Lock 4 — F-LOCK-HOLE** (Isolated day between FED blocks):
If an employee has FED blocks with an isolated non-FED day between them (e.g., FED days 1–3 and FED days 5–7, leaving day 4 blank), that isolated day is a mandatory working shift — mark it F-LOCK-HOLE. The weekly FOD quota must be placed on other available days in that week.

**Lock 5 — Contractual fixed FOD:**
Employees with contractual fixed off-days (extracted in STEP 3.3) receive FOD on those days every week. These are inviolable — mark them FOD-FIXED. They cannot be overridden by F-LOCK, coverage needs, or suplência.

**Lock 6 — Suplência F-LOCK:**
For each suplência rule extracted in STEP 3.3: on every day where Employee A has FED, AJD, COD, or BMD, Employee B (the backup) must work the designated shift. Mark Employee B's cell as F-LOCK = [specific shift code, e.g., B01]. This takes priority over Employee B's rotating FOD for that day.

Exception: if Employee B has a contractual fixed off-day (FOD-FIXED) on that day, contractual right prevails. Find the next available backup and flag:
`"ALERTA DE SUPLÊNCIA: O suplente designado de [A] tem folga contratual em [dia]. Aguardo indicação do próximo suplente disponível."`

**Then — assign rotating FOD days:**

For each employee, for each calendar week, execute in this exact order:

**STEP 0 — Calculate rotating_quota (mandatory before any placement):**
```
1. Does this employee have FOD-WEEKEND (Sáb+Dom) in this week?
   → YES: rotating_quota = 0. The 2 FODs are already placed. SKIP to next week.
   → NO: continue.
2. Count FOD-FIXED cells this employee has in this week → N_fixed
3. rotating_quota = 2 − N_fixed
   → rotating_quota = 2: place 2 rotating FODs
   → rotating_quota = 1: place 1 rotating FOD
   → rotating_quota = 0: quota satisfied by FOD-FIXED. SKIP to next week.
4. If week contains FED days: apply FED-WEEK REST DAY RULE (PART 4.2)

```

**STEP 1 — Identify valid slots** (only applies when rotating_quota > 0):
Valid days for rotating FOD: Seg, Ter, Qua, Qui, Sex — never Sáb or Dom (Lock 7).
Remove from candidates: F-LOCK, F-LOCK-HOLE, FOD-FIXED, Suplência F-LOCK, WORK-ONLY, NO-FOD, BLOCKED-FOR-B, FED.

**STEP 2 — Equity rule (apply before each FOD placement):**
1. Count the total number of FODs already placed on each valid day across ALL employees for that week.
2. Place the FOD on the valid day with the **fewest existing FODs** that week — this balances coverage across the week.
3. If two days are tied, prefer the earlier one in the week (Seg > Ter > Qua > Qui > Sex).
4. Sex is a last resort: only use Sex if all other valid days (Seg–Qui) are either locked, already at maximum, or would create a forbidden FOD pair.
5. Sáb and Dom: enforced by Lock 7 — NEVER use for rotating FOD under any circumstance.

**Maximum FODs per day per week:** No single weekday should have more than ⌈total_weekly_FODs / valid_days_available⌉ FODs across all employees. If placing a FOD on a given day would exceed this ceiling, move to the next least-loaded day.

Before placing any FOD on a given day, verify all of the following:
- Cell is not F-LOCK, F-LOCK-HOLE, FOD-FIXED, Suplência F-LOCK, WORK-ONLY, or NO-FOD.
- Working coverage will not drop below the **day-type minimum** after this FOD:
  - Seg–Qui: use the general daily minimum (from STEP 3.3).
  - Sex: use the Sex ⭐ minimum if specified separately in "Equipa e regras"; if not specified, use the general minimum. Sex is a high-volume day — apply the stricter value.
  - Sáb/Dom: enforced by Lock 7 (WORK-ONLY) — rotating FOD is never placed here regardless of coverage.
- This FOD on day D does NOT create a forbidden pair. Check explicitly:
  - Is D−1 already marked FOD, FOD-WEEKEND, FOD-FIXED, AJD, COD, or BMD? → consecutive rest → move to next priority day.
  - Is D+1 already marked with any rest code (same list)? → consecutive rest → move to next priority day.
  - Does the pair (D−1, D) or (D, D+1) match any forbidden pair: Sex+Sáb · Dom+Seg · Qui+Sex? → move to next priority day.
  - Would this FOD combined with the employee's FOD-WEEKEND in this week create a Sex+Sáb+Dom or Sáb+Dom+Seg block? → ABSOLUTE BAN — do not place even if Lock 8 marker was missed.
- For mutual-backup pairs: this day is not BLOCKED-FOR-B (Lock 9).

If all checks pass: place the FOD. If any check fails: move to the next priority day. If no priority day passes all checks: reduce week quota and report — never place FOD on a failing cell.

N_working + N_absent must equal total headcount (N from STEP 3.2). If they do not match: stop, recount both columns, identify the discrepancy, and fix before publishing the row. Do NOT publish a coverage number that fails this identity check.

**Coverage status per day:**
- Seg–Qui: compare N_working to the general daily minimum (STEP 3.3) → ✅ if ≥ minimum | ⚠️ if = minimum − 1 | ❌ if < minimum − 1
- **Sex: compare N_working to the ⭐ Sex minimum if specified in "Equipa e regras"; otherwise use the general minimum** — Sex is a high-volume day, apply the stricter value
- **Sáb and Dom: compare N_working to the ⭐ weekend minimum (STEP 3.3)** — use the weekend-specific floor, NOT the general minimum

Report format on "Resumo de Cobertura Diária" row: `[N_working] [✅/⚠️/❌]`

**NEVER estimate or calculate coverage by subtraction from a partial list.** Count working cells directly. Count absent cells directly. Verify they sum to total headcount. This rule is especially critical for Sáb and Dom where FOD-FIXED (contractual off-days) and FOD-WEEKEND are concentrated.

- **Always include** a "Totais do Período" table per employee (turnos trabalhados, total de horas, dias FED, dias FOD, dias COD).

**Out-of-scope requests:**

**Coverage Hard Stop:**
If coverage summary shows ⚠️ or ❌ for any day:
→ DO NOT output the final schedule.
→ `"BLOQUEIO: Cobertura insuficiente no(s) dia(s) [X]. Mínimo exigido: [N]. Disponível: [M]. Aguardo instrução do utilizador."`

A ⚠️ is a FAIL condition, not a warning to ignore.

**Suplência conflict:**
If the designated backup has a contractual fixed off-day on the day suplência is required:
`"ALERTA DE SUPLÊNCIA: O suplente designado de [A] tem folga contratual em [dia]. Indique o próximo suplente disponível ou ordene 'SOBREPOR DADOS: [Nome] trabalha [turno] em [dia]'."`

`"BLOQUEIO: Não consigo ler a folha '[Nome da Folha]'. Confirma se o ficheiro está acessível e se a folha existe com esse nome exacto."`

→ DO NOT generate a schedule.
→ `"BLOQUEIO: Não é possível cumprir simultaneamente o limite legal de 5 dias consecutivos do colaborador [X] e a cobertura mínima no dia [Y]. Aguardo instrução para forçar turno adicional ou reduzir mínimo nesse dia."`
