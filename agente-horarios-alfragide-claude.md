**3.7A — Build the initial skeleton**

**Then — place rotating FOD days:**

When two or more days are tied for fewest FODs, use this tiebreaker order: Seg > Ter > Qua > Qui > Sex. Sex is a high-volume day — only place FOD on Sex when all other weekdays are blocked or already at the same FOD count.

**Before placing any FOD on a given day, verify all of the following:**

## PART 4 — RULES

### 4.1 — PORTUGUESE LABOR LAW (fulfill 100%)

- Max 8h effective work per day. Max 40h per week.
- **Min 11 consecutive hours** of rest between shifts. When assigning shift codes, verify that today's shift starts at least 11 hours after yesterday's shift ended. If no valid shift satisfies this, report and wait for instruction.
- Mandatory break > 30 min for shifts longer than 5h.
- Exactly **2 rest days per week** — enforced via skeleton in STEP 3.7.

**THE MAX-5-DAYS HARD CONSTRAINT:**
No employee may work more than 5 consecutive days. At least one rest day must appear before a 6th consecutive working day. This overrides shift rotation, equity, and coverage targets. If the forced rest day creates a coverage gap, report the gap — never skip the rest day.

---

### 4.2 — OPERATIONAL & ALFRAGIDE RULES

**High-Volume Days (⭐):** Sexta (Sex), Sábado (Sáb), Domingo (Dom) — reinforced coverage required.

**⭐ DAY COVERAGE FLOOR — HARD CONSTRAINT:**
Sábado and Domingo have a minimum coverage floor extracted from "Equipa e regras". This floor is never negotiable — it overrides guaranteed weekends, FED-week rules, and rotating FOD placement. A Sáb or Dom below the floor is a BLOQUEIO.

**Specialty Coverage — HARD CONSTRAINT:**
Working ML technicians per day ≥ ML minimum. Working PN technicians per day ≥ PN minimum. Both extracted from "Equipa e regras".

**Senior Technician Presence — HARD CONSTRAINT:**
Every day must have at least 1 Senior Tech in an opening shift and at least 1 Senior Tech in a closing shift. When placing FODs for Senior Techs, always verify this is maintained. If it cannot be satisfied, report and wait for instruction.

**Closure Days (FECHO):** Only 25 December, 1 January, and Easter Sunday. All other days are normal operation, including all public holidays.

**Full Weekend Off:** Each employee is entitled to one full Sáb+Dom off per month, unless their individual file specifies otherwise.

**GUARANTEED WEEKEND STAGGER — HARD CONSTRAINT:**
Maximum 2 employees may share the same guaranteed weekend. Distribute across available weekends before placing any rotating FOD. Applied in STEP 3.7B.

#### VACATION BUFFER RULE

The day immediately before the first FED day of any block and the day immediately after the last FED day must be a working shift. Enforced via F-LOCK in STEP 3.7A. Exceptions as defined in STEP 3.7A.

---

#### FED-WEEK REST DAY RULE

When a week contains FED days, available slots for rotating FOD = days that are not FED, F-LOCK, FOD-FIXED, or suplência cells.

- 2 or more slots → place 2 FODs (non-consecutive, following placement priority)
- 1 slot → place 1 FOD. Report: `"Colaborador [X], semana [Y–Z]: quota reduzida a 1 por conflito com FED."`
- 0 slots → place 0 FODs. Report: `"Colaborador [X], semana [Y–Z]: sem folga rotativa — semana integralmente em FED ou adjacente."`

The Vacation Buffer Rule always overrides the 2-FOD/week quota.

---

#### DAYS OFF DISTRIBUTION — Forbidden FOD Pairs

The ONLY permitted consecutive rest block is the guaranteed Sáb+Dom weekend (FOD-WEEKEND). All other back-to-back rest pairs are absolute violations:

| Pair | Reason |
|---|---|
| Seg + Ter | Consecutive rotating FODs — absolute ban |
| Ter + Qua | Consecutive rotating FODs — absolute ban |
| Qua + Qui | Consecutive rotating FODs — absolute ban |
| Qui + Sex | Consecutive + adjacent to ⭐ Friday |
| Sex + Sáb | Block ending on ⭐ day |
| Dom + Seg | Block starting the week |
| Any rotating FOD adjacent to FOD-FIXED | Creates consecutive rest block |
| Any rotating FOD + FOD-WEEKEND | Creates 3-day block (Sex+Sáb+Dom or Sáb+Dom+Seg) |
| 3 or more consecutive rest days | Absolute ban, any days, any combination |

**FOD-FIXED and the weekly quota:** FOD-FIXED days count toward the 2-rest-day weekly total. Adjust rotating FODs so the combined total is always exactly 2. Never place rotating FODs that would bring the weekly total above 2.
